Paint-only frontline review

Red: provisional reference. Cyan: generated proposal. Yellow: your replacement line.

How to submit
To accept the cyan line, click Accept generated line. To reject it, click Reject generated line. Either action saves a versioned JSON decision.
To draw your own line: click Draw replacement, then click points along the line on the right image. Click Finish drawing, then Save my line. Use Undo or Clear if needed.
Red sketches are provisional. Check “Confirm reference” only if the red line is a fair human reference. Every saved decision records image, recording, polygon and classifier provenance.
