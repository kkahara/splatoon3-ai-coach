Define the battle line

Judge the clean frame first, including visible player positions. Draw the line as you understand it; it does not need to follow the paint edge. A difference from paint is not automatically an error. Prior sketches and paint predictions stay hidden until you save an independent judgment and choose Reveal comparison.

1. Your independent judgment

A battle line may curve, split into several lines, or be absent. Colored ink alone does not have to define it.

What influenced your judgment? (optional)

Choose a draw type and click points on the map. A line needs two points; a region needs three. Select a finished shape to erase it, or drag its white points to move them.

Each save creates a new revision; older judgments remain.
