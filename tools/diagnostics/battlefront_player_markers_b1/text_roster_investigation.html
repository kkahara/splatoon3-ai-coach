    Single roster-graphic comparison at 130s

    For one manually selected Orange-mark example, a grayscale multi-scale template search compared a cropped HUD weapon subregion (88×30 px) with the nearby map graphic. The best score was 0.733 at scale 0.40, at source location (769, 287), in the expected weapon-like mark above the human point. A full roster-card crop scored 0.586 at its best local match, at a different location. This is one exploratory pair, not an eight-player mapping, a calibrated similarity score, or proof of a stable transformation.

    Investigation limits

    • Scene-text methods produce candidate text regions, not proof that a region is a player name. HUD/notification exclusion and association with a marker still need evaluation.
    • CRAFT predicts character-region and character-affinity maps; that character-level formulation is relevant to short names, but does not guarantee detection of a single period at this image scale. CRAFT was inspected but not run because PyTorch and its pretrained weights are absent locally.
    • DBNet’s confidence is a model score, not a calibrated probability that a region is a player name. Japanese-name performance is outside the published TD500 English/Chinese scope.
    • Roster weapons and map-side symbols look related in some examples, but this page does not score a correspondence or claim a fixed scale. Matching needs independently cropped, unoccluded examples and negative comparisons.
    • Identity, team, and position remain separate questions. A matched weapon graphic can support a candidate without identifying a username or proving team.
