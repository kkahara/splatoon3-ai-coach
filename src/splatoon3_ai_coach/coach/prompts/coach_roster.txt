ROSTER GUIDANCE (present only when the roster evidence capability is on)

Architecture:
- PlayerCount samples = fused ally/opponent alive roster at labeled times
  (HUD roster-X fusion; not fight participation)
- PlayerCount window / context = compact roster timeline around the unit
  anchor, plus present_by / duration_since_present_by derived in coaching
  (not GameEvents; not ScenarioContext fields)

Evidence class:
- Player-count samples / window (ally_alive_count / opponent_alive_count)
   when present — roster state only. Prefer present_by wording:
   “Disadvantage was observed by 43.0s, 5.0s before the anchor.”
   Never say “the player was disadvantaged for 5.0 seconds” from
   duration_since_present_by alone — that field is a sampled presence bound
   (contiguous observations; holes larger than max lookup gap end the run),
   not continuous disadvantaged time or an exact transition instant.

Roster rules:
- Player-count samples describe the observed alive roster at a time. They do
  not establish which players participated in a particular engagement.
- Never infer alive counts from splat counts, death events, or scenario
  membership.
- A fused roster change (e.g. 4v4 → 3v4) is not proof that a particular
  teammate died, that a trade happened, or that the player should have
  disengaged. Cite roster state only as tactical context.
- Team roster counts describe the whole team, not a fight the player took.
  When death_while_outnumbered or death_while_ahead_in_numbers is active you
  may state the counts from roster.before_death, e.g. "Your team had 3
  players alive against 4 just before you were splatted." Do not describe
  it as a fight ("you fought outnumbered", "you took an outnumbered fight").
- A roster count is a circumstance, never a verdict. Never say the player
  should have pushed because allies outnumbered opponents, or should have
  retreated or played passively because opponents outnumbered allies.
- roster.before_death is the sample the roster factors used (taken just
  before the death). roster.at_death may already count the player's own
  roster X marker; prefer before_death when stating counts at the death.
- When describing roster.player_count_context, prefer:
  “{numbers_state} was observed by {state_present_by}s,
  {duration_since_present_by}s before the anchor.”
  Do not upgrade that into continuous disadvantaged time.

Roster importance factors (attention context, not claim cards):
- death_while_outnumbered: in roster.before_death, opponent_alive_count
  exceeds ally_alive_count by at least the configured gap (default 2).
  Statement only: state both counts. Not an outnumbered fight; no "should
  have retreated / played passively".
- death_while_ahead_in_numbers: in roster.before_death, ally_alive_count
  exceeds opponent_alive_count by at least the configured gap (default 2).
  Statement only: state both counts. It makes the death worth examining,
  not a mistake; no "should have pushed".
